# TaxZilla Company Context

TaxZilla is an ambitious tax strategy, bookkeeping, and financial advisory firm based in Ashland, Alabama, founded by Chris Lucas. The company specializes in comprehensive tax planning and preparation for high-income W2 earners and small business owners.

TaxZilla’s core philosophy is aggressive and empowering: turning clients from "Tax Prey" into "Tax Predators" by using tax strategy as an offensive weapon to plug financial leaks and build multi-generational wealth.

## Current Business Model

- Monthly subscription model with regular bookkeeping and end-of-year tax returns.
- The company is transitioning from a traditional, manual tax practice into a fully integrated, AI-empowered wealth management ecosystem.

## Organizational Structure

TaxZilla is founder-led by CEO Chris Lucas, who manages overarching strategy, client relationships, and core financial operations.

The business is organized around functional processes instead of large corporate departments:

- Tax & Bookkeeping Operations
  - Handles monthly logging of expenses, mileage, and end-of-year tax returns.
  - Currently relies heavily on manual data entry from pictures of receipts and mileage logs sent directly to the founder's phone.

- Client Experience & Portal Management
  - Oversees client interactions, currently decentralized across text messages, emails, and shared Google Workspace folders.

- Marketing & Brand Development
  - Projects the "King Zil" persona and builds a cohesive brand presence.

- Wealth Strategy & Advisory
  - Represents the future expansion, focusing on analyzing tax leaks and redirecting savings into wealth-building strategies.

- Technology & AI Infrastructure
  - The connective tissue being built out with Google Workspace as the base.
  - Mandate is to build automated workflows, OCR integrations, and eventually proactive AI advisory agents.

## Current State and Challenges

- Passionate founder and validated subscription model.
- Lacks technological infrastructure to scale.
- Manual receipt logging and lack of a centralized client portal are major operational bottlenecks.

### Immediate Focus

- Automate baseline bookkeeping and data collection.
- Build a strong, branded web presence.
- Create a secure client portal connected to Google Workspace.
- Once the data pipeline is clean, deploy AI agents to scan financial ledgers and identify tax leaks and wealth-building opportunities.
